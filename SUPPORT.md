# Support

- Report a problem or ask a question: https://github.com/SeongRaeNoh/shalltrack/issues
- Please include the page or tab where it happened, what you expected and what you saw. Do not post confidential RFP or proposal text; a short invented example is enough.
- ShallTrack is a Codegeist 2026 entry maintained by one person. Answers are best effort, usually within a few days.
- Security issues: open an issue that says only "security report" and how to reach you; the details can then be exchanged privately.
